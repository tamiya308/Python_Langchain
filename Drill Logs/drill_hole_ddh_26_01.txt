SECTION 2: SAMPLING AND ASSAY RESULTS

Half core samples were cut on site with an automatic core saw and submitted to
the laboratory in Perth. Samples were dried, crushed to 2mm and pulverised to
85% passing 75 microns. Gold was analysed by 50g fire assay with an AAS finish.
Multi-element analysis used a four-acid digest with ICP-MS finish.
